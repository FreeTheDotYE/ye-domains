This collection preserves public HTTP responses for historical research,
accountability, and study of the `.ye` namespace. Inclusion documents a public
response and does not imply endorsement, ownership, authorship, or control by
FreeTheDotYE.

grant a license to third-party archived content and does not waive any rights,
exceptions, limitations, or obligations that apply under relevant law.

Researchers and redistributors are responsible for evaluating the lawful basis
for their own use. Preserve attribution and context where appropriate, avoid
misrepresenting historical responses as current, and do not use the collection
to target individuals or compromise systems.

## Data and tooling

The manifest, aggregate summary, checksums, and validation tooling describe the
archive package. Their publication does not change the rights status of the
embedded third-party responses.

## Privacy and corrections

The public package removes collection-bound timestamps, cookies, request IDs,
forwarding data, and similar metadata. Public response bodies are intentionally
preserved as evidence and may contain information placed online by the
originating site. Metadata normalization cannot recall third-party downloads or
caches.

For a specific, well-founded privacy, safety, or rights concern, contact
`contact@freethedotye.org` with the affected domain and archive filename. A
request will be reviewed in context; this notice does not promise a particular
legal outcome or removal decision.
